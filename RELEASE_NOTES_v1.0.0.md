# SAC-SQ-Plus v1.0.0

First public research-artifact release accompanying *Collective Exclusion Certificates for Complete Regularization Paths under Interval-Valued Segment Costs*.

Included:
- R333 original fitted-cost acquisition pipeline and its complete imported dependency closure.
- R343 updated Occupancy n=1024 source snapshot and full archived output.
- R372 certificate-bank-pricing source and both Occupancy n=256 archived workload arms.
- R357 fixed-cell multi-witness enumeration, R390 exact continuous witness-interval certification, and R354 exact closed-support filtering.
- R381 250-case independent exact exhaustive validation.
- R382 six-arm pairwise-only ablation.
- R385 m=8 uniform-interpolation separation.
- R390 1,000 random candidate-inclusion tests, 11-box summary, and n=256 15-repeat same-host benchmark summary.
- R392 archive-independent witness generation and 11 frozen fitted-cost boxes.
- R393 same-count and singleton-transition analytical constructions.
- R399 independent-witness paired benchmark (135 pairs / 270 executions) with raw logs and exact outputs.
- UCI download/preprocessing scripts, frozen X/y windows, archived acquisition outputs, five R400 manuscript figures, and their numerical backing tables.

Source code/scripts are MIT; authors' derived numerical data and figures are CC BY 4.0. Original UCI raw archives are fetched from UCI by the included script. The upstream SKAB dataset and SKAB-specific derived matrices are not redistributed; its stress logs and source citation are retained. See `DATA_SOURCES.md`, `DATA_LICENSE.md`, and `docs/SKAB_REPRODUCTION.md`.
